- Open the clipboard in your chosen position with either the shortcut or a left-click. Right-click and Control-click open the options menu.
- Settings opens on General, and Settings, About and the welcome screen consistently show the ClipVee icon.
- See when history is paused, the next copy will be skipped, or capture is turned off, with actions to resume or open the relevant settings.
- Prevent copies made while paused from being captured after a quick resume, and make skip-next-copy apply to the next new copy.
