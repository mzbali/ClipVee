- The search field is shown at the top of the panel by default. Click it or just start typing; it no longer shows a blinking caret until you do.
- Choose in Settings → Panel whether the search field is shown always, only while you type, or never. With Never, typing no longer starts a hidden search.
- Press ⌘V in the panel to paste the selected card, just like Return or a click.
- Clear History… has moved into the panel's … menu, and the menu items now have icons.
- Buttons in the panel show a pointing-hand cursor.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later on Apple silicon or Intel. This release is not notarised by Apple; the installation guide explains the first-launch approval step.
