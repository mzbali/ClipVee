- Copying or pasting an item from the panel no longer moves it to the top of the list. It keeps its place, even among items with the same number of copies.
- When the list is sorted by last copied, copying something in another app still puts it at the top, whether it's new or already in your history.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later on Apple silicon or Intel. This release is not notarised by Apple; the [installation guide](https://mzbali.github.io/ClipVee/#install) explains the first-launch approval step.
