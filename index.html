Your clipboard stays on your Mac.

ClipVee runs in Apple's app sandbox without network access, so the app itself can't reach the internet. No account, no analytics, no cloud.

  • Passwords copied from password managers are skipped
  • Ignore whole apps, pause history, or skip just the next copy
  • The only thing that goes online is a separate updater, which asks GitHub for the latest version once a day, if you allow it
  • Everything is open source, so anyone can check

Install in a minute

ClipVee is free, so it isn't notarised by Apple. macOS asks you to confirm it once.

Download ClipVee, open the disk image, and drag ClipVee to Applications. Or install it with the Homebrew command at the top.
Open ClipVee. If macOS says it can't check it, click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
Allow Accessibility. In the welcome window, click Allow… and turn ClipVee on in Privacy & Security so it can paste for you. Then press ⌘ ⇧ V.

Using Maccy? ClipVee can bring over your history and pins. Prefer to build it yourself? It takes one command.

Questions

Why does macOS warn me when I first open it?

Apple charges developers a yearly fee to notarise apps. ClipVee is a free project and isn't notarised yet, so macOS asks you to confirm it once with Open Anyway. The full source is on GitHub if you'd like to check it or build it yourself.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

To paste for you, ClipVee presses ⌘ V in the app you were using, and macOS only allows that with Accessibility turned on. Without it, ClipVee still copies the card you choose, and you paste it yourself.

Is it really free?

Yes. ClipVee is open source under the MIT licence. There's no paid version, account or subscription.

Does my history sync to my iPhone or other Macs?

No. Your history stays on this Mac. ClipVee only goes online to check GitHub for a new version, and only if you allow it.

How do I update it?

ClipVee asks whether to check for updates automatically. If you say yes, it looks once a day. When a new version is ready, an update button appears in the panel and in the menu bar icon's menu, and it installs when you click it. You can also click Check now in Settings → General. Your history, pins, settings and Accessibility permission carry over. Version 1.0.1 and earlier can't check, so download the new version once and drag it to Applications.

Can I change the shortcut?

Yes, in Settings → Shortcuts. You can also choose whether picking a card pastes it or only copies it.

How is it related to Maccy?

ClipVee is built on Maccy, a popular open-source clipboard manager by Alex Rodionov. It keeps Maccy's clipboard capture, storage and privacy features, and replaces the menu-style list with a Windows-style panel of cards that pastes for you.
